140th Canton Fair — Guangzhou, China

The China Import & Export Fair is the world's largest trade fair, run twice a year in Guangzhou. The Spring session runs from 15 April to 5 May and the Autumn 2026 session (the 140th) runs from 15 October to 4 November 2026, each across three phases at the Pazhou Complex. Agha Travel & Tours is facilitating business visas for Pakistani importers, buyers and exhibitors attending.
