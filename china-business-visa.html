About The Fair

What Is the Canton Fair?

The China Import and Export Fair (Canton Fair) is the largest trade fair in China, held twice a year in Guangzhou. It brings together thousands of exhibitors and international buyers across electronics, machinery, consumer goods, textiles, and more.

  • Venue: China Import and Export Fair Complex, Pazhou, Guangzhou
  • Session: 140th Canton Fair, Autumn 2026
  • Dates: 15 October – 4 November 2026, in 3 phases
  • Who attends: Importers, wholesale buyers, exhibitors & trade delegations
  • Visa needed: China Business (M) Visa
